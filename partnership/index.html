Partnership

Structured collaboration for system deployment and improvement

What is a Partner?

A partner is an institution or organization that uses Wekomere Systems while contributing to system development through real-world usage, feedback, and operational collaboration.

Partners vs Clients

Partners

Collaborate in system use and development

Provide feedback and operational insight

May receive early or flexible access

Clients

Use established systems for operations

Access stable and structured services

Typically operate under standard service terms

What Partners Gain

Access to structured system tools

Improved operational processes

Early access to system features

Opportunity to influence system development

What Partners Contribute

Active system usage within their operations

Feedback for system improvement

Operational collaboration during deployment

Optional financial or technical support

How Partnership Works

Expression of Interest → Initial Discussion → System Setup → Usage & Feedback → Continuous Improvement

Partnership Terms

Partners manage their own operational data

System use is for structured service delivery

Feedback supports ongoing system improvement

Terms may evolve as systems develop
