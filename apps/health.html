Digital Health Intelligence (DHI)

Transforming clinics and drug shops into intelligent health systems

What is Health Intelligence?

Health Intelligence is a digital system that helps clinics and drug shops manage patients, drugs, records, and reporting using real-time data instead of manual books and paper files.

The Problem in Clinics Today

Manual patient records are easily lost

No real-time drug stock tracking

Paper-based prescriptions slow down service

No data for decision-making or planning

How Digital Health Intelligence Works

1. Digital Patient Records

Every patient gets a digital profile stored securely in the system.

2. Smart Prescriptions

Doctors record treatment digitally with instant history tracking.

3. Drug Stock Management

Clinics track medicine levels and receive low-stock alerts.

4. Clinic Intelligence Dashboard

See trends: common diseases, patient flow, and drug usage.

5. Reporting Automation

Generate health reports instantly for management or government.

System Flow

Patient visits → Digital registration → Diagnosis → Prescription → Drug dispensing → Data stored → Intelligence generated

What Clinics Gain

Faster patient service

Accurate medical history

Reduced drug wastage

Better decision-making using data
